Scope: `/home/korvin/apps/traiNNer-redux-dev` (the owner's fork of the-database/traiNNer-redux; upstream merged
2026-10-21). Read: the shipped templates (`options/_templates/train/**`, 400 files, generated by
`scripts/options/generate_default_options.py` from `scripts/options/train_default_options_*.yml` and the
per-arch tables `OFFICIAL_SETTINGS_FROMSCRATCH/FINETUNE` in `traiNNer/archs/arch_info.py:518-1225`), the owner's
own configs (`options/train/**`), all 54 `experiments/*` dirs (3.7 MB of logs, 255 validations), `README.md`,
`architecture_guide.md`, `tables_template.md`, `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`, `docs/source/{training_guidelines,
benchmarks}.md`, `docs/iterative_loss_scheduling.md`. CLAUDE/AGENTS and the LLM-written docs
(`llm_sisr_best_practices.md`, `codex-2.md`, ParagonSR2 readme) were read as statements of intent only: they
contain no measurements.

Evidence labels used below: **[probe]** = measured here (tiny capped CPU probe, see section 3.1); **[log]** =
the owner's traiNNer logs; **[cfg]** = shipped configs; **[paper]** = published result, not re-measured here;
**[reasoning]** = mechanism only.

## 0. Verdict

1. **There is no evidence that traiNNer's recipes or trainer train better than neosr at equal architecture.**
   Every traiNNer run on the owner's data is ESRGAN (16.7 M params) or a custom arch; every neosr A/B is
   realplksr (7.4 M). On the one validation set neither side trained on (`val8`, 7 images), traiNNer's
   L1-led ESRGAN is +0.35-0.6 dB PSNR over neosr's L1 realplksr at 5-8k updates, but neosr's new GAN template is
   *more perceptual* than every traiNNer GAN run at the same update count (DISTS 0.159 vs 0.188-0.191, TOPIQ
   0.637 vs 0.603-0.622) at -0.8 dB PSNR [probe]. Both gaps are confounded by the arch. One cheap A/B (neosr
   ESRGAN template, 8k updates, scored with the probe below) settles it; do that before porting any recipe.
2. **The owner's own traiNNer logs say perceptual quality came from feature-space losses, not from the GAN.**
   Within one run (same ESRGAN, same history), scored with TOPIQ (independent of every training loss): +5k
   updates of VGG perceptual 0.1 + LDL 0.5 lifted TOPIQ 0.640 -> 0.706; +19k updates of r3gan GAN alone left
   it flat (0.706 -> 0.707) while DISTS got worse (0.143 -> 0.165); +6k updates of ConvNeXt 0.2 + DISTS 0.15
   lifted it 0.707 -> 0.745 at flat PSNR [probe]. The r3gan term was ~1% of the generator loss value, and at
   weight 0.1 it caused 0.8 dB PSNR dips [log]. This independently confirms the stage-2 direction (feature
   loss is the perceptual driver, the GAN is a small extra), and makes **ConvNeXt perceptual and DISTS-as-loss
   the only loss-side candidates with on-data evidence** (DISTS loss already exists in neosr: config-only A/B).
3. traiNNer's ViT-feature recipe failed on the owner's data the same way neosr's old FDL(dinov2) 0.75 template
   did: in `Experimental3B` the DINOv3 perceptual term was ~95% of the loss value, `grad_norm_d` sat at
   400-600 and PSNR collapsed to 14.0 dB at 19k [log]. Keep feature losses small (stage-2 FDL 0.02 is right).
4. The shipped templates are a uniform, unablated recipe (MS-SSIM 0.5 + VGG 0.01 + HSLuv 1.0 + vanilla GAN 0.1,
   AdamW 1e-4 both nets, dunet D, pretrain required) plus per-arch paper settings for lr/batch/patch/warm-up.
   The only parts worth taking are the **per-arch settings table** (light archs at 5e-4..1e-3, transformers at
   patch 64 with accumulation and warm-up) and the **fidelity + GAN template pair with a pretrain-first guide**.
   Both are data/doc, not code, and each wants one A/B on the owner's data first.
5. Usability worth copying: a short training guide, fidelity templates, `grad_norm_g/d` in the periodic log
   line, a config diff against the template in the run log, a fixed OTF validation-set generator, and TOPIQ
   (not DISTS) as the selection metric when DISTS/FDL are training losses. Everything automatic
   (DynamicLossScheduler, AGC, adaptive_d, intelligent LR/early stop, dynamic batch/patch, OOM auto-shrink,
   zero-config) stays out: all 74 of the owner's traiNNer launches that logged the banner (78 logs) had
   automations off, so there is not even anecdotal evidence for them.

## 1. Shipped recipes (templates) [cfg]

Generator: one paired fidelity base, one paired GAN base, two OTF fragments; per arch, `%lr%`, `%lq_size%`,
`%batch_size_per_gpu%`, `%milestones%`, `%total_iter%`, `%warmup_iter%` come from the paper tables
(`arch_info.py:518-864` from scratch, `:865-1222` fine-tune; `""` is the default row). Betas `[0.9, 0.99]`,
EMA 0.999 are hard-coded defaults (`generate_default_options.py:126-127`); fp16 AMP and channels_last are on
except for listed archs (`ARCHS_WITHOUT_FP16`, `ARCHS_WITHOUT_CHANNELS_LAST`).

| recipe | traiNNer template | neosr template (stage 2) |
|---|---|---|
| stages | `*_fidelity.yml` (from scratch) and `*_gan.yml` (needs `pretrain_network_g`), each paired and OTF | one GAN template per arch (paired, OTF), GAN from update 1, no pretrain |
| G optimizer | AdamW betas (0.9, 0.99), wd 0; lr from table: 2e-4 heavy/medium, 5e-4 SPAN/OmniSR/MAN/HiT-SRF/ESC/ATD-light, 1e-3 LKFMixer/CascadedGaze; fine-tune 1e-4 | AdamW 2e-4 for every arch |
| D optimizer | AdamW 1e-4 | adan_sf 1e-4 |
| schedule | MultiStepLR, gamma 0.5, paper milestones (e.g. 250k/400k/450k/475k of 500k); GAN (fine-tune table): e.g. 125k/200k/225k/237.5k of 250k, realplksr 100k of 250k | none (constant), 1M updates |
| warm-up | -1 except ATD 10k/20k, ESC 20k | none in templates (supported) |
| fidelity loss | Charbonnier 1.0 only | n/a (no fidelity template) |
| GAN mix | MS-SSIM 0.5, VGG perceptual 0.01 (charbonnier criterion), HSLuv 1.0, GAN vanilla 0.1; DISTS/LDL/FF/cosim/FLIP/Sobel listed at weight 0 | L1 1, MS-SSIM 1, consistency 1, LDL 1, FDL(dinov2) 0.02, GAN bce 0.3 |
| D | dunet | metagan |
| EMA | 0.999, `ema_power` 0.75 on fidelity templates | 0.999, `ema_power` 0.75 |
| patch / batch | lq 64-96; batch 4 (ATD, HAT) to 16 (light), `accum_iter` comment with paper batch | lq 64, batch 8; **lq 32 for all 26 transformer templates** |
| AMP | fp16 default, bf16 per arch | bf16 + fast_matmul |
| clip | off (max norm 100 if on) | on, max norm 1.0 |
| validation | fidelity: PSNR/SSIM; GAN: TOPIQ | metrics commented out (PSNR/SSIM/DISTS/TOPIQ examples) |
| batch aug (MoA) | listed, off | off (stage 2 decision 11) |

Observations:
- No template weight in the GAN mix is backed by an ablation in the repo, the docs or the logs. HSLuv 1.0 in
  the templates vs 0.1 in every owner run; VGG perceptual 0.01 is tiny next to Charbonnier-scale terms.
- The per-arch table is copied paper settings for DF2K bicubic training (2x from scratch, 4x fine-tune from the
  2x model). It is the most reusable artefact of the template system: it encodes facts neosr's uniform
  templates ignore (light nets train at 2.5-5x the lr of heavy nets; transformer papers use LQ 64 patches).
- For the owner's data the GT tiles are 256 x 256 (LQ 64), so LQ 64 is the whole tile; neosr's transformer
  templates use a quarter of each tile (LQ 32), with 2 x 2 windows of size 16 per patch.

### 2.1 Cross-evaluation on common sets [probe]

Script `scratchpad/recipes_lane/cross_eval.py` (CPU, fp32, 4 GB cap): loads saved generators into neosr's
`esrgan` / `realplksr` (old-style `model.*` ESRGAN keys remapped), runs inference, scores every model with the
same neosr metric code (PSNR/SSIM RGB crop 4, DISTS, TOPIQ-FR). Pipeline check: neosr Q8 val67 29.41 / 0.1783
here vs 29.36 / 0.1787 in its own log; traiNNer 7k val8 29.161 / 0.7498 / 0.1908 here vs 29.164 / 0.7492 / 0.1907
in its log. Outputs: `scratchpad/recipes_lane/cross_eval_{all,phases}.json`.

`val8` = 7 images nobody trained on (fair). `val67` = neosr's held-out 67, which **were in traiNNer's training
set**; the traiNNer models' val67-minus-val8 gap (1.21-1.51 dB) is at most ~0.25 dB larger than that of neosr's
L1 model (1.27 dB), so contamination is worth roughly 0-0.25 dB to them. TOPIQ was never a training loss for any model; DISTS was a loss for the 43k model and
FDL/DISTS-like features drive neosr Q8, so DISTS is partly circular, TOPIQ is the independent judge.

| model (arch, updates) | objective | val8 PSNR / SSIM / DISTS / TOPIQ | val67 PSNR / SSIM / DISTS / TOPIQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| neosr P0 (realplksr 8k) | old template (FDL 0.75, adan_sf, hard clamp) | 26.79 / 0.645 / 0.160 / 0.578 | 27.27 / 0.628 / 0.202 / 0.571 |
| neosr R3 (realplksr 5k) | L1 only, AdamW 2e-4 | 28.65 / 0.735 / 0.203 / 0.520 | 29.91 / 0.746 / 0.228 / 0.555 |
| neosr Q8 (realplksr 8k) | new template (shipped now) | 28.39 / 0.719 / **0.159** / **0.637** | 29.41 / 0.722 / 0.178 / 0.679 |
| traiNNer (ESRGAN 7k) | charb + HSLuv 0.1 + r3gan 0.01, RpGAN-scalar D | 29.16 / 0.750 / 0.191 / 0.603 | 30.53 / 0.762 / 0.208 / 0.649 |
| traiNNer (ESRGAN 8k, batch 16) | charb + HSLuv 0.1 + r3gan 0.05-0.1, metagan3 D | 29.25 / 0.752 / 0.188 / 0.622 | 30.69 / 0.765 / 0.206 / 0.667 |
| traiNNer main 13k | r3gan 0.1 phase (in a dip) | 28.59 / 0.741 / 0.177 / 0.640 | 29.80 / 0.750 / 0.196 / 0.694 |

What this supports:
- At equal updates (~8k) neosr's template sits at a different perception-distortion point than the owner's
  traiNNer GAN runs: better DISTS and TOPIQ, 0.8 dB lower PSNR, lower SSIM. A model that is ahead on one axis
  and behind on the other is not "worse"; and part of the PSNR gap is the arch (next point).
- L1-led training: traiNNer ESRGAN is +0.35-0.4 dB over neosr realplksr L1 at 5k on val8 (log values at 5k:
  28.98-29.06 vs 28.65), +0.5-0.6 dB at its 7-8k checkpoints. Same data, same patch and batch, but 2.3x the parameters and ~1.4x the per-update compute
  (stage-2 benchmark: esrgan 137 ms vs realplksr 95 ms). Not attributable to the trainer.
- Within-run deltas (same arch and history, TOPIQ val8): VGG+LDL phase +0.066, GAN-only phase +0.001 (and DISTS
  +0.022 worse), ConvNeXt+DISTS phase +0.038 at flat PSNR. The best traiNNer model (43k) is ahead of neosr Q8 on
  every metric, after 5x the updates and three loss phases; that says "long training with feature losses
  works", not "traiNNer's trainer is better".

What it does not support: anything about traiNNer's shipped template mix (never run on this data), about
realplksr in traiNNer, or about seeds (one run per arm).

## 6. What neosr already does better

- The objective is balanced by measurement (term-gradient census, FDL 0.02) and every template default was
  flipped by an A/B on the owner's data; no traiNNer template weight has an ablation behind it.
- At ~8k updates on the owner's data, the new neosr template is more perceptual (val8 DISTS 0.159, TOPIQ 0.637)
  than every traiNNer GAN run the owner made (0.188-0.191, 0.603-0.622) [probe; arch-confounded].
- The A/B protocol (held-out split, seeded init and data, EMA eval, one variable per arm) vs traiNNer's
  edit-YAML-and-resume phases with no held-out data, which made attribution impossible without this probe.
