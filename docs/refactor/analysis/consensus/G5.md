| G5.1 | TRUE | medium | fix-now | judge | ldl_loss.py:92-94 (and duplicate loss_util.py:29-31): `torch.var(residual_sr.float(), dim=(-1,-2,-3), keepdim=True).clamp_min(1e-12) ** (1/5)`.
